Plain-Language Guide

What You Need to Know as a Guarantor

Acting as a guarantor is a serious legal commitment. We believe in educating our clients and their guarantors so decisions are made with complete clarity.

A guarantor promises to repay the outstanding facility if the principal borrower defaults.
Guarantors must be over 21, possess verifiable regular income, and have a healthy credit history.
You have the right to receive notice and understand the exact liability amount prior to signing.
